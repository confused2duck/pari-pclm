What Pari is not

  • A generic CMMS with a crane skin
  • A maintenance log people fill out after the fact
  • A dashboard that shows what already broke
  • Free-text root cause fields nobody can query
  • A compliance folder on a shared drive

What Pari is

  • A domain model that understands duty class, FEM/ISO classification, and component taxonomy
  • An engine that turns every failing inspection item into a corrective action, automatically
  • Pattern detection that catches repeat failures no human reviewing individual work orders would
  • Structured root-cause capture (5-Why, fishbone) — queryable, benchmarkable, ML-ready from day one
  • A state-jurisdiction-aware compliance calendar with one-click auditor reports

Module 02 · Inspection Engine

Every finding becomes a corrective action, without re-entry.

Checklists are data — Checklist → Section → Item, versioned. A FAIL on brake lining condition forces a mandatory air-gap measurement. A response above a configured severity opens a corrective action assigned to the right role, dated against the right SLA. No back-office triage.

  • Versioned templates — historical inspections stay pinned to the version used
  • Conditional follow-ups drive complete-and-correct capture
  • Parallel checklist types per crane: daily / weekly / monthly / annual / statutory / commissioning

Module 05 · Repeat-Failure Intelligence

Catch the pattern no engineer has time to notice.

A background job on every event checks failure count on the same component_id within a rolling window (default 120 days, per-criticality configurable). When it fires: total downtime, ₹ production loss from the plant's configured cost rate, and a mandatory root-cause investigation opened to the Reliability Manager.

  • Designed from day one to be ML-ready, not bolted on later
  • Cross-tenant benchmark snapshots, anonymized and opt-in
  • The seed data for future failure-prediction models — structured, not free text
